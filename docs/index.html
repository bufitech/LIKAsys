What it does

It does one job well.

You get the numbers you care about, in the corner, without opening anything. That is the whole idea.

Always on top

The card stays above games, above the browser and above everything else. You can also make it click through, so the mouse goes straight past it.

Only in the tray

No button on the taskbar and nothing in alt tab. It sits next to the clock, and that icon can show CPU, GPU, RAM, FPS or temperature as a number. Right click opens a menu that turns white when Windows is in light mode.

Real FPS

The FPS comes from Windows itself, the way professional tools read it. You also get 1% low and frametime, so the stutters show up too.

Update with one click

Press Update in the settings. The new version downloads, installs and the app opens again on its own. It also checks every 6 hours and tells you when something is ready.

Change what you want

63 themes, 4 icon styles, any font you have on the PC, 9 positions, and sliders for corners, opacity, blur and shadow. Or pick a profile and leave it alone.

Free

No price, no subscription, no ads, no account. It sends nothing anywhere. The only time it goes online is to see if there is a new version.

The views

Two profiles, many looks.

When you install it, LIKAsys asks one question: gaming or work. After that it sets up the rows, the icons, the font and the colours by itself. You can switch any time from the tray icon.

Mouse

Four pointers, only in IT.

The IT profile has its own mouse settings. Four sets of pointers, each one different. A set changes the arrow, the text cursor, the link cursor and the crosshair, all four at once.

NeonDark glass with a cyan edge that glows. It goes with the dark themes.arrow, text, link, crosshair
TerminalGreen phosphor pixels with scan lines. The text cursor becomes a console block.arrow, text, link, crosshair
CarbonSolid metal with a white hairline all around it. Quiet, clean, no glow.arrow, text, link, crosshair
BlueprintA technical drawing. Thin lines, diagonal hatching, a blue target.arrow, text, link, crosshair

You will find them in Settings, Mouse, or with a right click on the tray icon. The pointer changes everywhere in Windows while LIKAsys is open and your own pointer comes back when you close the app. Nothing is written to the registry.

Questions

What people ask.

Is it really free?
Yes. There is no paid version, no subscription and no ads. You do not need an account or an email.
Why does it need administrator?
For two things: reading the FPS from Windows and reading the temperature sensors. Everything else works fine without it.
Does it slow games down?
No. There is no overlay inside the game, it does not hook into DirectX and it injects nothing into your processes. It is a normal window that stays on top.
How does the update work?
Open Settings, then About, then press Update. It downloads, installs and opens again. Your theme, position and settings stay the same.
Does it work with two monitors?
Yes. Choose the monitor and the position, or drag the card with the mouse. It stays where you leave it after a restart.
Can I change the language?
Yes, English and Albanian, from the settings. Nothing needs to restart.
Where are the settings saved?
In your user folder, in a small text file. Nothing goes to a server.
How do I remove it?
From Add or remove programs in Windows, like any other app.
