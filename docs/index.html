What it does

One thing, done very well.

LIKAsys does not pretend to be a control centre. It is a small card that tells you exactly how hard your machine is working - instantly, without taking your eyes off what you are doing.

Always on top

The single most important thing this program does. The card stays above a full-screen game, above the browser, above everything - it never slips behind. It can even be made click-through, so the mouse passes straight through it.

Tray only

It takes no space on the taskbar and never shows up in alt-tab. It lives in the icon next to the clock - and that icon can draw CPU, GPU, RAM, FPS or temperature as a live number.

Real FPS

Not a guess. LIKAsys reads the actual frame stream from Windows itself, the way professional tools do - together with 1% low and frametime, so the stutters show up too, not just a pretty average.

One-click update

Open the settings, press Update and that is it. The new version downloads, installs and the app restarts itself. It checks quietly every 6 hours and only speaks up when there is really something new.

Yours, down to the corner

45 themes in 8 groups, 4 icon styles, any installed font, 9 ready positions, opacity, blur, corners, shadow, bars, and vertical / horizontal / compact layouts. Or just pick a profile and never think about it again.

Free. Full stop.

No payments, no subscription, no ads, no account, no 'pro' version. It collects nothing and sends nothing - the only time it touches the internet is to see whether a newer version exists.

The views

Two profiles. Dozens of looks.

At install time it asks one single question: gaming or work? From there it sets itself up completely - the rows, the icons, the type, the colours, the corners, even the way it arrives on screen. Switch any time with one click from the icon next to the clock.

Questions

What everyone asks.

Is it really free?
Yes, forever. No 'pro' version, no subscription, no ads and no account. Not a single piece of personal data is required, not even an email.
Why does it ask for administrator rights?
For two things only: reading the frame stream (FPS) from Windows and reading the temperature sensors. Without administrator everything else works normally, but FPS and temperatures cannot be read - that is how every tool of this kind works.
Does it slow the game down?
No. There is no in-game overlay, it does not hook into DirectX and it injects nothing into your processes. It is an ordinary Windows window that stays on top, with a single process and a practically unmeasurable load.
How does updating work?
Open Settings › About › Update. The app checks whether a newer version exists, downloads it, installs it and restarts itself. Your settings, theme and position stay exactly as they were. The automatic check runs every 6 hours.
Does it work with two monitors?
Yes. Choose the monitor and the position - 9 ready spots or a completely free one by dragging with the mouse. The card stays where you leave it, even after a restart.
Can I change the language?
Yes - English and Albanian, switched instantly from the settings, with no restart. The installer itself asks which language you want before anything else.
Where are the settings stored?
In your own user profile, as a plain text file. Nothing goes to any server and nothing is hidden in the registry.
How do I uninstall it?
Like any other program - from Add or remove programs in Windows. It leaves nothing behind.
